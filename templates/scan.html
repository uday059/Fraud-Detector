🛡️ Score Level Meaning

The risk score represents the level of suspicious indicators identified during the static APK analysis.

Score Level Meaning
0–20 🟢 Very Low Risk No significant suspicious indicators found
21–40 🟢 Low Risk Few indicators detected
41–60 🟡 Medium Risk Some suspicious indicators require attention
61–80 🟠 High Risk Multiple suspicious indicators detected
81–100 🔴 Critical Risk Strong suspicious indicators detected
⚠️ Important: A higher score means more suspicious indicators were identified. This is a static-analysis assessment and does not guarantee that an APK is safe or malicious.
